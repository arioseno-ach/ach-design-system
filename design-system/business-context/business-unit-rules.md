# Business unit rules

Compliance language, legal constraints, and unit-specific limits. UI and copy must not contradict this file.

## Language and claims

- Do not promise outcomes the product cannot guarantee.
- Do not give legal, tax, or medical advice in UI copy unless this unit is authorized to.

## Data and consent

- Collect only fields required for the current flow.
- Required legal disclosures stay visible; do not bury them only in a dialog.
- Marketing opt-in is never pre-checked.
